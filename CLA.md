## Why this exists

VitaCore is [dual-licensed](LICENSING.md): GNU AGPL v3 for everybody, and a
commercial licence for organisations that cannot use AGPL software.

Offering both requires holding rights broad enough to grant both. A
contribution received under the AGPL alone could never appear in a
commercially licensed copy, and the second licence would quietly stop being
offerable. This agreement keeps that from happening.

Nothing here restricts what you may do with your own work. You can use it
elsewhere, relicense it, or build on it, exactly as if you had never
contributed it.

## How to sign

Two things, both once:

**Per commit — the Developer Certificate of Origin.** Every commit carries a
`Signed-off-by:` line, which is you confirming section 5. Add it
automatically:

```bash
git commit -s -m "your message"
```

**Once per contributor — this agreement.** On your first pull request, add a
comment saying:

> I have read CLA.md and I agree to it.
> Signed: Your Name <your@email>

That is the whole process. There is no form to print and no document to post.

## Corporate contributions

If you are contributing on behalf of an organisation and somebody there needs
to agree on its behalf rather than you personally, open an issue titled
"Corporate CLA" and a maintainer will arrange it.
